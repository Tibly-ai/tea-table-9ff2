Sensory offerings & gatherings

Our curated experiences are designed for small groups seeking mindful connection, technical mastery, or a meditative space to mark the passing seasons.

Guided Tea Flights

An intimate sit-down tasting led by our tea steward. Choose between rare oolongs, aged pu-erh, or delicate spring green teas. Includes traditional Gaiwan brewing techniques and dynamic flavor profiles.

2–6 Guests From $45 pp

Blending Workshops

Learn the foundational art of blending. Work with organic botanicals, dried herbs, and whole-leaf bases to compose your own signature tin of tea. Perfect for creative duos and milestone gatherings.

4–10 Guests From $60 pp

Seasonal Solstice

Quarterly silent tea ceremonies focusing on transition, stillness, and seasonal tea transitions. Sip high-elevation mountain teas in restorative silence, ending with quiet group sharing and reflection.
